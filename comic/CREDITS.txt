BLQ mobile comic print assets
=============================

Derived from the existing cleared assets in public/objects and public/photos.
Their source credits remain in those directories. These add no new source art.

The original SVG duotone, gamma, contrast and registration-shadow treatments
are rendered once at up to 3x the comic artboard resolution, capped at each
source's native detail, then stored as lossless WebP with transparency. The
live composition retains its separate objects,
photo masks, halftone dots, type, choreography and beat response.

Recipes: scripts/comic-assets-source.json
Rebuild: node scripts/bake-comic-assets.mjs
Requires Node 22+, Chrome (or BROWSER_BIN), and Python Pillow.
