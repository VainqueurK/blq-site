Story music
===========

The journey plays from public/audio/tracks/ as one continuous shuffle with
programmatic equal-power crossfades (3.8s overlap in lib/audio.ts, dual-deck
streaming crossfades on mobile and Web Audio highpass-cleared crossfades on
desktop). Tracks are loudness-normalised to ~-14.5 LUFS with dead leading and
trailing whitespace trimmed (preserving quiet intros and low risers) and no
baked fade-out:
